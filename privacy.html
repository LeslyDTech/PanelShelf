Privacy Policy

PanelShelf

Effective date: September 16, 2026

PanelShelf is a personal collection tracker for comic issues, graphic novels, and manga. This Privacy Policy explains what information PanelShelf handles and how it is used.

Information PanelShelf Handles

  • Email address and authentication information used to create and secure an account.
  • Collection details you add, such as titles, issue numbers, publishers, statuses, and notes.
  • Optional cover images you upload for collection items.
  • An anonymous, aggregate member count displayed on the home page.

How Information Is Used

PanelShelf uses this information to sign you in, save and display your personal collection, provide optional cover searches, and keep the app working. Your collection is not displayed publicly to other PanelShelf users.

Services That Help Run PanelShelf

PanelShelf uses Supabase for account authentication, database storage, and image storage. When you use cover search, PanelShelf sends your search terms to the Google Books API to look for a possible cover image.

Sharing and Selling Data

PanelShelf does not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the services needed to operate the app, such as Supabase and Google Books, as described above.

Security

PanelShelf uses account authentication and database access rules intended to keep each user’s collection separate. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and keep your account details private.

Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your PanelShelf account and its associated collection data at any time. Send the request from the email address connected to your PanelShelf account. Do not include your password in the message.
